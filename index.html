Final-year project, rebuilt for production

A safety app that stays one tap away from help.

Stay Secure is an Android app that gives women instant access to emergency contacts, one-tap SOS alerts, live location sharing, and a plain-language guide to their legal rights — built with Flutter, PHP, and MySQL.

View source on GitHub See the app

What it does

Built around the moments that matter most

Every screen answers one question: how fast can a person in distress get help, and how easily can the people around them find out?

✓

Status at a glance

A single SAFE indicator on the home screen, so the user's own status is never more than a glance away.

⚠

SOS features

Live location sharing, a safe-route suggestion, quick camera access, and background video recording, all from one panel.

☎

Emergency contacts

Police, Women's Helpline, Child Helpline, and Ambulance numbers pre-loaded, plus two personal emergency contacts per user.

⚖

Know your rights

A plain-language summary of key Indian laws for women's safety — IPC sections explained without legal jargon.

🛡

Self-defense guide

Practical guidance a user can review ahead of time, not something they have to search for in the moment.

👤

Simple profile & auth

Straightforward registration and login backed by a PHP + MySQL API, with a clean profile view of saved details.
